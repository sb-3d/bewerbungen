## some general information about me

I grew up in Munich. After school, I completed an apprenticeship as a heating engineer. In 1991, I graduated from the Technical University of Munich with a degree in technical physics (Dipl. Ing.).

I have always had a strong connection to Denmark, especially to the island of Als, where close friends of mine lived. In the summer of 2025, my family and I moved to Kegnæs. My daughter now attends school in Sønderborg.

I am passionate about my work and enjoy developing my skills and exploring new possibilities. I consider myself friendly, open-minded, and reliable. I work with dedication and integrity as part of a motivated team. I embrace new challenges and strive to complete tasks successfully.
